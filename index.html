FAQ

Do you really buy broken phones?

Yes. Cracked screens and even devices that won't power on are eligible. We'll give you a quick quote after a short check. Photo ID is required.

How long does an iPhone screen repair take?

Most iPhone screens are in stock and finished same day, typically within 45-90 minutes depending on volume.

What does the 6-month repair warranty cover?

Parts and workmanship for the specific repair we performed. It excludes liquid damage, drops/cracks after the repair, and third-party tampering.
